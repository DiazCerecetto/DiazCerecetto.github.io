Can you tell how many people are on the bus before getting on?

With data from the Intendencia de Montevideo, passengers on the 21 Portones bus at 6 p.m. at 18 de Julio and Arenal Grande can be estimated from the cars passing along 18 de Julio in 30 seconds: p ≈ −0.8v2 + 10v + 16. An idea similar to Dolbear's law, which estimates temperature by counting cricket chirps.
